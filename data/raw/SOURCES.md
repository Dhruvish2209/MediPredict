- **Source:** UCI ML Repository / Kaggle
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uciml/pima-indians-diabetes-database
- **Original UCI URL:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/34/diabetes

  Using the ADAP learning algorithm to forecast the onset of diabetes mellitus.

## 3. Heart Failure Prediction Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle (compiled by fedesoriano from 5 UCI datasets)
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fedesoriano/heart-failure-prediction
- **Rows:** 918 | **Features:** 11
- **License:** CC BY 4.0
- **Original UCI Index:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/machine-learning-databases/heart-disease/
- **Combined from 5 hospitals:**
  - Cleveland: 303 observations
  - Hungarian: 294 observations
  - Switzerland: 123 observations
  - Long Beach VA: 200 observations
  - Stalog (Heart): 270 observations
  - Total after dedup: 918 observations
- **Citation:** fedesoriano. (September 2021). Heart Failure Prediction Dataset.
  Retrieved [19-11-2026] from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fedesoriano/heart-failure-prediction
- **Original donors:** Andras Janosi, M.D.; William Steinbrunn, M.D.;
  Matthias Pfisterer, M.D.; Robert Detrano, M.D., Ph.D.
